================================================================================
                    ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE IN EXAM PREPARATION
================================================================================

1. OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Artificial Intelligence (AI) has shifted exam preparation from passive rote 
memorization to adaptive, active recall systems. By leveraging Natural Language 
Processing (NLP), Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and spaced repetition 
algorithms, modern AI tools act as 24/7 personal tutors tailored to individual 
learning paces.

2. CORE CAPABILITIES IN EXAM STUDY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Personalized Diagnostics & Gap Analysis:
  AI evaluates practice test performance, detects recurring conceptual errors, 
  and dynamically generates targeted practice sets for weak topics.

- Automated Active Recall & Flashcard Generation:
  Converts lengthy lecture PDFs, textbooks, or syllabus documents into active-
  recall flashcard decks and spaced-repetition schedules (e.g., Anki, Quizlet).
