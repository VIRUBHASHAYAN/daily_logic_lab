================================================================================
                         AI IN VIDEO EDITING: A GUIDE
================================================================================

1. CORE AI TECHNOLOGIES IN VIDEO EDITING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Computer Vision: Object detection, face tracking, green-screen replacement, 
  and automated scene segmentation.
- Machine Learning (ML): Automated cut detection, pacing adjustments, rhythm 
  matching, and style transfers.
- Natural Language Processing (NLP): Speech-to-text transcription, automated 
  captions/subtitles, and text-based editing (deleting filler words or 
  rearranging clips via text scripts).
- Generative AI: AI B-roll generation, background extension (aspect ratio 
  resizing), and object removal (generative fill).

2. KEY WORKFLOW SHIFTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- From Technical to Conceptual: Tedious frame-by-frame masking and tracking 
  are reduced to single-click selections.
- Text-Based Editing: Editing a video by reading and modifying its written 
  transcript rather than scrubbing a timeline.
- Automated Subtitles: Instant generation of dynamic captions with word-level 
  timestamps and karaoke-style highlights.

3. POPULAR TOOLS & PLATFORMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Runway / Codex / Remotion: Natural language and code-based video manipulation.
- FireCut / Palmier Pro / Gemini Omni Flash: Automated rough cuts, talking-head 
  reframing, and motion graphics.
- Traditional NLEs with AI (Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve): Native AI color match, 
  voice isolation, and transcript-based editing.
