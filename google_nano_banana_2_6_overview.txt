1. WHAT IS "NANO BANANA"?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Nano Banana" is the public arena alias and development codename for Google's 
reasoning-based visual generation model suite (built directly upon the Gemini 
multimodal and Flash architecture, such as Gemini 3.1 Flash Image and Gemini 
Pro Image).

The name gained prominence in AI evaluation arenas (like LMSYS / Arena benchmarks) 
before and during its integration into Google AI Studio, where models were tested 
under anonymous codenames before formal branding.

Note on the "2.6" Designation:
- "Nano Banana 2" designates the second-generation reasoning image engine 
  powered by Google's Gemini 3.1 Flash architecture.
- In creative workflows and benchmark comparisons, "2.6" often appears due to 
  comparisons with Alibaba's Wan 2.6 multimodal suite, or as an interim release 
  tag denoting point-revision updates to the Nano Banana 2 pipeline (incorporating 
  improved temporal consistency, higher token limits, and enhanced text rendering).


2. CORE ARCHITECTURAL CAPABILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Unlike traditional purely diffusion-based models, Nano Banana models leverage 
Gemini's transformer-based reasoning backbone to "think" and simulate scene 
composition, physical relationships, and prompt constraints before pixel synthesis.

Key Highlights:
- Reasoning-Guided Generation:
  Simulates lighting physics, object gravity, and relative scale prior to 
  rendering.
- Native Typography & Text Adherence:
  Renders crisp, legibly spelled text across UI mockups, signs, posters, and 
  menus in multiple languages.
- Iterative & Conversational Editing:
  Supports multi-turn conversational edits (e.g., "now change the character's 
  jacket to leather while keeping facial features identical").
- Search & Knowledge Grounding:
  Capable of referencing real-world landmarks, biological details, and niche 
  objects accurately via integrated knowledge retrieval.
- SynthID Watermarking:
  Every generated frame carries Google's invisible SynthID watermark for 
  provenance and AI identification.


3. TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS & INPUT/OUTPUT MODES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Parameter / Feature          Specification / Details
-------------------          ---------------------------------------------------
Backbone Model               Gemini 3.1 Flash Image / Pro Multimodal
Input Modalities             Text prompts, Reference Images, PDF documents
Context Window               65,536 to 131,072 input tokens
Supported Aspect Ratios      Up to 14 standard aspect ratios (1:1, 16:9, 9:16, etc.)
Native Resolutions           0.5K, 1K, 2K, and 4K native output
Generation Latency           Fast Flash-tier response (~2 to 10 seconds)
Output Format                High-resolution PNG / Lossless streams
Safety & Watermarking        SynthID embedded at generation level


4. COMMON USE CASES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Consistent Character Storyboarding:
  Maintains up to 90-95% facial and wardrobe identity across multiple scenes 
  and camera angles.
- Commercial & Product Mockups:
  Generates production-grade product visualization, packaging design, and 
  e-commerce banner assets.
- UI/UX & Infographic Layouts:
  Creates structured diagrams, dashboards, and presentation templates with 
  accurate label positioning.
- Style Translation:
  Converts reference artwork across distinct styles (e.g., photorealistic, 
  isometric 3D render, watercolor, anime) while preserving core subject geometry.


5. DEVELOPER INTEGRATION EXAMPLE (GOOGLE GENAI SDK)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Using Python with the official Google GenAI SDK:

    from google import genai
    from google.genai import types

    client = genai.Client()

    prompt = (
        "A hyper-realistic studio product shot of a sleek glass bottle "
        "with embossed typography reading 'Nano 2.6', soft ambient studio "
        "lighting, 4K resolution, 16:9 aspect ratio."
    )

    response = client.models.generate_content(
        model="gemini-3.1-flash-image",  # Nano Banana 2 engine
        contents=[prompt]
    )

    for part in response.parts:
        if hasattr(part, "as_image"):
            image = part.as_image()
            image.save("output_nano_banana.png")
